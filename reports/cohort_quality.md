## Cohort contamination

**Thermal (SAGD/cyclic steam): EXCLUDED from targets (2026-08-26).** 8,942 licences flagged via WellStatusType SAGD/CYCL or self steam injection; they remain as neighbours, where their steam is context with the correct sign. Reasons: steam-driven pace is not comparable to primary recovery, the nearest neighbour is often the well's own injector twin at 0 m, and the play proxy is mostly missing in thermal country.

**Gas-storage operators: EXCLUDED from targets (2026-08-26).** 107 storage licences remain in the cohort as geometry/neighbours with NULL targets; before exclusion their median target was ~4,155% of peer P50 -- withdrawal of injected gas, not production.

**Commingled pools: EXCLUDED from targets (2026-08-26).** 95,409 licences in COMMINGLED pools remain as neighbours/score-only. Several wells report as one stream, so the per-well pace label is an allocation artifact: before exclusion their P99 target was 2,499% vs 1,270% for everyone else, and they took 37% of the top-100 extreme targets from 15.6% of the population.

**Targeted wells with unsurveyed bores (leg-count floor): 475.** Their `n_legs` understates reality; `n_bores_reported` carries the honest count.
